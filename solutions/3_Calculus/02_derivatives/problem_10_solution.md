# Problem 10 — The Tangent Line as an Application of the Derivative

[Exercise sheet](../../../exercises/3_Calculus/02_derivatives_en.md)

## Problem

For

$$
f(x)=x^2-3x+1
$$

find the equation of the tangent line at the point with $x=2$. Give the point of tangency, the value $f'(2)$, the equation of the line, and verify that the point lies on the obtained tangent line.

## Method

A tangent line at $x=a$ is $y-f(a)=f'(a)(x-a)$.

## Solution

The point of tangency and slope are

$$
f(2)=4-6+1=-1,\qquad f'(x)=2x-3,\qquad f'(2)=1.
$$

Thus

$$
y+1=x-2,\qquad y=x-3.
$$

**Result:** the tangent touches at $(2,-1)$ and has slope $1$. Substituting $x=2$ into the line gives $y=-1$, so the point lies on it.

## Verification

The difference between the curve and line is $x^2-3x+1-(x-3)=(x-2)^2$. It vanishes to second order at the point of tangency.
